Neutral Eats — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Neutral Eats is a restaurant finder built on a simple idea: no ads, no bias, just food. That idea extends to your data. This policy describes what the Neutral Eats iOS app and the neutraleats.com website and API do — and don't do — with information.

No accounts, no sign-up

Neutral Eats has no user accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no profile to create, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information to use the app.

No tracking, no analytics, no advertising

The app contains no analytics SDKs and no advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, we do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell or share information for advertising. The app contains nothing designed to identify you.

How map requests work

To show you places, the app sends our API the part of the map you are currently viewing — a set of bounding-box coordinates — along with anything you type into search and any category filter you choose. That is the entire request: it carries no name, no account, and no device identifier, and it is not linked to you as a person.

Your precise location stays on your device. The app uses it, with your permission, to center the map — but it never sends your GPS coordinates to our servers. What it sends is the region on screen; if you pan the map to another city, the region sent is that city. (If you choose to center the map on yourself, the region you are viewing will, naturally, be in your vicinity.)

Tips

If you choose to leave a tip, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple as an in-app purchase through the App Store. We never see, receive, or store your payment card details. Apple may provide us with aggregated sales information (for example, total tip counts), which contains no payment details.

Server logs

Like most web services, our servers keep standard operational logs — IP address, timestamps, and the URLs requested — which we use only to operate the service, keep it secure, and fix problems. These logs are retained briefly and are not used to build profiles or for marketing.
